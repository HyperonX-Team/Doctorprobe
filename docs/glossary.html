Glossary

The vocabulary of the project, from the chemistry to the infrastructure. Terms are grouped by area.

Chemistry & analysis

Analyte
A substance measured by the test - here glucose, CRP, cortisol, pH, and secretory IgA.
Biomarker
An analyte reported with a value, unit, reference range, and state (low/normal/high).
Beer-Lambert law
Absorbance is proportional to concentration: A = -log10(R/R₀). The physics behind strip colorimetry.
Chromogen
The coloured reaction product a reagent pad develops; its intensity encodes concentration.
Reference range
The literature-plausible normal interval used to classify a value as low/normal/high.
Identifiability
How confidently an analyte's concentration could be separated from the other channels. Reported as a 0-1 confidence.
NNLS
Non-negative least squares - the solver finds concentrations ≥ 0 that best fit the measured absorbances.
Tikhonov prior
A regularization term that steadies ill-conditioned directions toward a sensible estimate (SaliNet's prediction).
Condition number
A measure of how ill-posed the reconstruction is; high values mean channels are weakly separated.
Reconstruction residual
How much of the measured signal the fitted model fails to explain; used in quality grading.

System

Checkup
One analysed reading: a snapshot of all five biomarkers, encrypted at rest.
Burst
A cluster of snapshots of the same strip captured within ~45 s; the solver deconvolves them together.
Blank baseline
An unstained-strip capture (CAL BLANK) that defines a device's "white" for gain correction.
Fernet
Symmetric encryption (AES-128-CBC + HMAC) used to encrypt report payloads at rest.
Bearer token
An opaque session credential issued at login/register; sent as Authorization: Bearer ….
Session digest
The SHA-256 hash of a token that is actually stored, so a DB leak exposes no usable tokens.
Quality grade
good / fair / poor verdict on a capture, from burst variance, light levels, and solver conditioning.
Prior source
Whether the solver's prior came from SaliNet (salinet) or the closed-form Beer-Lambert fallback (beer-lambert).

Machine learning

SaliNet
The sensor model: four per-analyte Random Forests that invert RGB+temp+humidity into concentrations.
Forward model
The simulator (Beer-Lambert + pad cross-talk + noise) that generates synthetic training data.
Calibration sample
A labeled capture from a control standard with known concentration, used to retrain SaliNet on reality.
Manifest
salinet.json - records model version, training time, per-analyte source, and R²/MAE.
Provenance
Per-analyte record of whether training data was real or synthetic and how many samples.

Hardware

TCS34725
The I2C RGB colour sensor that reads the strip; exposes clear (unfiltered) + RGB channels.
DHT22
Temperature/humidity sensor used for enzyme-kinetics compensation.
ESP32
The microcontroller brain with Wi-Fi; runs the Arduino firmware.
Normalized channel
An RGB channel divided by the sensor's clear channel, making readings lighting-independent.
CAL mode
A firmware state that labels the next capture with an analyte + concentration for training data.
